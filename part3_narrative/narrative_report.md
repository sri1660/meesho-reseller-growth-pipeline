# Chart-Choice Justification

## 1. Which month had the highest total revenue?

**Recommended chart: Vertical bar chart.**

This is a univariate comparison of total revenue across April, May, and June. A vertical bar chart makes the highest month immediately visible within a few seconds. The Y-axis should start at zero to avoid exaggerating differences, and no 3D effect is necessary.

The verified monthly totals are:

* April: INR 419417.43
* May: INR 444594.25
* June: INR 398055.24

Therefore, **May had the highest total revenue**.
